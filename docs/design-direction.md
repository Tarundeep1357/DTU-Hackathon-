# Evidence review workspace

Design references consulted before implementation:

- [Elicit](https://elicit.com/): question-led research, extracted evidence, inspectable quotations, and structured review. Adapt the relationship between a claim and its supporting extract.
- [Linear](https://linear.app/): compact persistent workspace navigation and clear separation of activity from record properties. Adapt navigation density and reading hierarchy, without reproducing its branding.
- [NotebookLM](https://notebooklm.google/): source-oriented research context. The public homepage redirected during research, so it was not used as authority for exact colors or component geometry.
- Refero bundled `typography.md`, `color.md`, and `craft-details.md`: neutral work-tool typography, restrained semantic color, labelled controls, visible keyboard focus, and readable excerpts.

Locked direction: a charcoal navigation rail, off-white reading canvas, muted blue actions, narrow borders, restrained corner radii, and generous whitespace around evidence text. Keep status words visible; color never carries a verdict alone. Do not add decorative stock images, gradients, or a marketing dashboard.

The review flow is intake → extraction → claims → findings → source inspection → gap test. The source inspector shows page location, exact extracted text, page appearance for PDFs, and integrity metadata. Hypotheticals stay in a separate, labelled lab.
